# Script shapes

Pick one per video in step 2 of the skill; the video's pillar (`AGENTS.md`) picks it. Each shape keeps the user's voice and the checklist; what changes is the format (`DESIGN.md`, Formats), the spine (what carries the viewer from start to end), what counts as a peak, the length, and the ending. Add a shape here when a new kind of video earns one.

## News

Pillar: AI info (AI news, AI in general, AI startups).

Something just happened: a launch, a startup, a study, an attack (Korea's banks, Black October). Start high, then stack small peaks.

- **Format**: full-screen animation (format 3), opening on the first animated shot.
- **Opening**: the news itself, present tense, as if already in motion, led by its stakes: who gets hurt, what breaks, what is at risk. The most-viewed reels opened on a threat (Korea's banks hacked, the AI virus, Black October).
- **Spine**: what it is → how it works ("So how does it work?") → the numbers → what it means. When the news is a chain of events (Korea, Black October), tell the events in time order first and save the user's opinion for the end.
- **Peaks**: a surprising number, a contrast with what people assume, a reveal. The first one lands by about 10 seconds.
- **Doubt beat** (optional): one skeptical line the fact table backs ("but that's what they said; nobody has checked it independently"). It counts as a peak. Write one when the user's draft or opinion carries the doubt; balancing lines of your own ("to be fair…") get cut (1022).
- **Length**: 45 to 60 seconds (about 150 to 190 words).
- **Ending**: a personal line ("I just hope…"), then "Comment X" for the full breakdown.

## Journey

Pillar: education about AI and technology.

How something works (Anatomy, the TV-box sharding). Follow one thing from start to finish.

- **Format**: full-screen animation (format 3) or split screen (format 2, Anatomy).
- **Opening**: the thing, in the viewer's own life, mid-journey ("Right now, a beam of light is carrying a photo your mom just sent.").
- **Lens**: one device that lets the viewer see inside and tells them where it ends: slowing 200 ms down 750 times with a millisecond counter on screen, special glasses that show radio waves. It becomes a visual that stays on screen.
- **Spine**: the one thing (the photo, the song, the model's answer) moving step by step.
- **Peaks**: each step meets a new problem and solves it: "But this data is too big…", "But that creates a new problem…". A problem re-hooks better than a date or "then".
- **Plant and payoff**: name a detail early that matters at the end ("Remember those packet numbers").
- **Length**: up to about 2 minutes 30, only while every step brings a new problem; otherwise cut to the steps that do.
- **Ending**: zoom out with a callback to the opening or a then-and-now contrast (1858: 16 hours for 98 words; today: 0.2 seconds). No "Comment X" unless the user wants one.

## Personal

Pillar: personal opinion (productivity, the user's own apps, their life).

The user's own experience or opinion.

- **Format**: face cam (format 1), opening on the face; pictures only where a point needs one.
- **Opening**: drop into the moment of tension, in first person, present stakes ("I have six months to ship this app, and nobody has used it yet.").
- **Spine**: what the user wanted → what stood in the way → what they tried → the moment that changed their mind → what they do now.
- **Peaks**: each try that failed or surprised, and the turn where what they believed broke against what happened (the contrast).
- **Lesson**: one thing the viewer can do today, said twice: once short, once as the user's own example.
- **Truth**: every detail comes from the user (step 1's `[ask]` list). Keep their real numbers, names and order of events.
- **Length**: 45 to 75 seconds.
- **Ending**: a callback to the opening line, with where the user stands now. "Comment X" only when there's something to send.
