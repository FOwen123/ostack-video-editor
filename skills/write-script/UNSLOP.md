# Unslop: not sounding like AI

The general layer of the voice: what makes any script sound machine-written once it's spoken. It holds for every creator. [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) is the creator's own layer and wins where they disagree: a phrase the user really says stays, even if it's on this list.

Read each line as the user would say it on camera, and rewrite every line that hits a pattern below, keeping its meaning and its facts.

## Openers and turns

- **Announcing instead of saying:** "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "Here's the catch", "Buckle up", "Let that sink in", "In today's video", "Picture this", "Imagine a world where". Say the thing itself.
- **Fake suspense** answered in the next breath: "The result? Chaos." Keep a question only when it's the one the viewer is already asking.
- **Filler hype as glue:** "But that's not all", "And it gets better". Keep one only when a bigger point follows (checklist, Anticipation).

## Shape

- **Triplets for rhythm:** "faster, cheaper, smarter". Use as many items as the facts give.
- **"Not just X, it's Y"** and "This isn't X. It's Y." Say Y.
- **Same-shape sentences:** every line the same length and build. Mix short lines with one that runs long.
- **A moral after every section:** "And that changes everything." End on a fact or a line the user would really say.
- **Recaps** of what was just said.

## Words

- **AI vocabulary:** game-changer, revolutionary, groundbreaking, unlock, unleash, harness, leverage, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, landscape, realm, delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, navigate, ever-evolving, supercharge. Use the plain word, or the number.
- **Hype without a number:** "massive growth", "huge funding". Give the figure.
- **Vague sources:** "experts say", "many believe", "studies show". Name who, or cut it.
- **Stacked hedges:** "could potentially", "may possibly". One hedge, or none when the source is sure.
- **Words nobody says out loud:** utilize, thus, moreover, furthermore, additionally, whilst, numerous, in conclusion.

## Said aloud

- **Breath:** a sentence that can't be said in one breath gets split.
- **Brackets and stacked clauses:** spoken lines have none. One idea per sentence.
- **Nouns doing a verb's job:** "the implementation of the model" becomes "they built the model".
- **Borrowed intimacy:** "I'll be honest with you", "Let me tell you", "Smart people like you".

## Last pass

Ask which line a viewer would screenshot as "ChatGPT wrote this". Rewrite it, and ask again until no line qualifies.
